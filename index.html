Reliability

PIT is where each truth fell in the native forecast distribution. Flat means calibrated, a U shape means intervals too narrow, a hump means too wide, a tilt means bias; the band is a rough 95% range for a flat histogram. Reliability plots empirical against nominal coverage over several α, for the native quantiles and the focused method (each extra α reruns that method on the same pairs). The larger dot is the current α. Uses the leads picked in the ranking.
